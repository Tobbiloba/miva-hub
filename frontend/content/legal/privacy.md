## 1. Who We Are

Askly is an AI-powered academic assistant for Nigerian university students. We are operated by Askly and governed by Nigerian law. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in accordance with the Nigerian Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

**Contact:** support@asklyhq.com

This is also the contact for all data subject requests under the NDPA.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

### Account Data

When you sign up, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Password (stored hashed, never in plain text)
- Academic program and level
- Matric number (optional)
- Current semester and academic year

### Usage Data

As you use Askly, we collect:

- Chat conversations with AI (your messages and AI responses)
- Study activity (materials viewed, flashcards reviewed, AI tools used)
- Progress data (streaks, completion rates)
- Notification interactions

### Payment Data

Payments are processed by Paystack. We receive:

- A tokenised authorisation code (not your card number)
- Customer code
- Transaction reference and amount

We **never** receive or store your full card number, CVV, or card PIN.

### Technical Data

For security and abuse prevention:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Timestamps of access

## 3. Why We Collect It (Lawful Basis)

Under the NDPA, we process your data on the following bases:

| Basis | What it covers |
|-------|---------------|
| **Contract performance** | Providing the service you signed up for: AI tutoring, flashcards, progress tracking, billing |
| **Legitimate interest** | Security, fraud prevention, abuse detection, product improvement, service analytics |
| **Consent** | Marketing communications, if we add them in the future (you will be asked explicitly) |

We do not process data for purposes beyond what is described in this policy.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

- **Deliver the service:** power AI responses, track your progress, send transactional emails (welcome, billing, notifications)
- **Personalise AI responses:** your chat history and enrolled courses are used to give you relevant answers
- **Prevent abuse:** detect fraudulent accounts, enforce rate limits, protect system integrity
- **Improve the product:** aggregate usage patterns (not individual data) to identify which features are useful
- **Legal compliance:** respond to lawful requests from Nigerian authorities

## 6. Educational Materials Disclosure

**This section describes the educational content available through Askly. Read it carefully.**

Askly's AI features are powered in part by educational materials sourced from MIVA Open University's learning management system. These materials are collected by volunteer contributors who are enrolled MIVA students using a browser extension we provide. We do not have a formal licensing agreement with MIVA Open University to redistribute these materials. We use them under the principle of student-to-student educational sharing, and we will remove any specific material on valid request from the copyright holder.

These materials are stored on our servers (AWS S3, US-East-1 region). They are not collected by Askly staff directly — they are contributed by student volunteers who have legitimate access to the LMS as enrolled students.

If you are a rights holder and want material removed, contact us at **support@asklyhq.com**. We will respond within 7 business days.

## 7. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account data | Kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request. |
| Chat conversations | Kept while your account is active. Deletable on request before account deletion. |
| Study activity & progress | Kept while your account is active. Deleted with account. |
| Billing records | 7 years after the transaction, as required by Nigerian tax law. |
| Technical/security logs | 90 days. |

After account deletion, we retain only what is legally required (billing records for tax purposes). Everything else is permanently deleted within 30 days.

## 8. Your Rights Under the NDPA

The Nigerian Data Protection Act 2023 gives you the following rights:

- **Right to access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- **Right to object:** Object to processing of your data for specific purposes.
- **Right to data portability:** Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to lodge a complaint:** File a complaint with the Nigerian Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data rights have been violated.

**To exercise any of these rights:** Email support@asklyhq.com with "Data Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity and respond within 7 business days.

---

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

- **In transit:** All connections use HTTPS (TLS encryption).
- **At rest:** Database (PostgreSQL) and file storage (S3) use encryption at rest.
- **Passwords:** Hashed using bcrypt. We cannot read your password.
- **Access control:** Data access is limited to authorised operators on a need-to-know basis.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the NDPC within 72 hours as required by the NDPA.

## 10. Cookies

We use **essential cookies only**:

- **Session cookie:** Identifies your logged-in session. Required for the service to function.
- **Authentication cookie:** Remembers that you are signed in across page loads.

We do **not** use:

- Marketing or advertising cookies
- Third-party tracking cookies
- Analytics cookies (for v1)

Essential cookies cannot be disabled without breaking the service. By using Askly, you accept these essential cookies.

Your data is processed and stored in the United States:

- **OpenAI servers** (United States): AI processing

The NDPA permits international data transfer where it is necessary for contract performance (providing you the service you signed up for). We rely on this basis for cross-border transfers. Our service providers maintain industry-standard security practices.

## 12. Children & Minors

You must be at least **16 years old** to use Askly.

If you are between 16 and 17 years old, the NDPA classifies you as a minor and requires parental or guardian consent for processing your personal data. By signing up, you confirm that:

- You are at least 16 years old, and
- If you are under 18, you have obtained the consent of a parent or guardian for your data to be processed as described in this policy.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we discover we have data from a user under 16, we will delete their account and data promptly.
